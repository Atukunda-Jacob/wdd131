W05 Project: Website Planning Document

1. Site Name

Kampala Fresh Market

Logo: 🥬 Kampala Fresh Market (simple text logo)

I chose this name because it represents a local online hub for fresh produce, vendors, and market prices in Kampala. It's relevant, short, and easy to remember. Domain availability: kampala-fresh-market.org

2. Site Purpose

The site provides a hub for Kampala shoppers and vendors by providing vendor information, daily market price lists, seasonal produce guides, and a contact/membership form for vendors to join the market network.

3. Scenarios

  • What are the current prices for tomatoes and matooke in Nakasero Market today?
  • Where can I find contact information for organic vegetable vendors in Kampala?

4. Color Schema

I am using my selected colors on this document as required.

Color Hex Use
Primary - Forest Green #1A5D1A Headings, header background, primary buttons
Secondary - Market Gold #F4A300 Accents, borders, call-to-action highlights
Background - Warm Beige #F9F5EB Page background
Text - Dark Gray #333333 Paragraph text

5. Typography

Heading Font: Montserrat Bold - Used for all h1, h2, h3. It is bold and modern for a market brand.

Example: Fresh Produce Every Day

Body Font: Open Sans Regular - Used for all paragraphs, lists, and general text. Very readable on mobile.

Example: Welcome to Kampala Fresh Market, your daily source for market prices.
